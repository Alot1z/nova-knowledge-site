The context addon. It decides what survives a token budget before anything reads it. It is not part of NOVA and adds zero parameters to it.

<strong>NOVA does not parse, summarise or compress context.</strong> It has no text model. It scores options. Feeding it less text changes the inputs; producing less text is a different job, and conflating the two is how a router ends up making a savings claim nobody measured.

Hypothesis: A zero-parameter deterministic planner can cut a long real context to a small budget without discarding the material a reader asked for, and can prove that with a retention probe.

Decision

MEASURED, and scoped to what was measured: on repo-corpus-v1 (153,008 bytes, 40,090 tokens by tiktoken) a 75.0% mean reduction at a 25% budget costs 89.2% mean probe retention (worst 50.0%), planning in 0.4595s mean / 0.5985s worst with 0 added parameters. NOT a claim about downstream answer quality, NOT a production token-savings figure, and the reduction is exact only insofar as the counting method (exact tokenizer) is. Gate A11 stays open for end-to-end savings because an option's real cost and latency are still priors until production telemetry lands.

Limit

These numbers describe how much of this repository's own text survived a budget on reader-shaped queries. They are not a token-savings claim about any workload, and gate A11 remains open. An option's cost and latency are priors until a running loop records real outcomes.
