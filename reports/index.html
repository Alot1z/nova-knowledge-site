Internal technical reports about NOVA. Each one asks a question, states the setup, shows the measured numbers, and says what those numbers do not license.

These are not peer-reviewed publications, and they cite no invented work. Every figure is read from the experiment files at build time, so a report cannot drift from the measurement it describes. Every accuracy figure is measured against world-analytic-v1, the analytic teacher NOVA was distilled from — a ranking claim, not a production claim.

That a learned scorer beats a fixed router at all, and the protocol every later experiment inherits.

Report 5: head to head against fixed routers

Report 6: head to head against bandits

The honest comparison, because a bandit only learns from outcomes.

Report 8: the context addon
